# MM/GBSA Binding-Energy Analysis

This directory contains scripts for processing and analyzing MM/GBSA binding-energy results for Thiazole Orange bound to G-quadruplex DNA.

## Energy Components

The analysis may include:

- van der Waals energy
- Electrostatic energy
- Polar solvation energy
- Nonpolar solvation energy
- Total estimated binding free energy

The calculated binding energy is expressed as:

ΔG_bind = G_complex − G_receptor − G_ligand

## Analysis Goals

MM/GBSA analysis is used to:

- Compare TO binding in K⁺ and Na⁺ environments
- Identify the dominant energetic contributions
- Evaluate differences among independent replicates
- Calculate cumulative or running-average binding energies

The calculated values are interpreted as comparative binding-energy estimates rather than absolute experimental free energies.
