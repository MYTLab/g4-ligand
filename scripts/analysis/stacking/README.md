# π–π Stacking Analysis

This directory contains scripts for analyzing π–π stacking interactions between Thiazole Orange (TO) and terminal guanine residues of the G-quadruplex.

## Method

The aromatic system of TO is divided into two ring groups:

- Benzothiazole ring
- Quinoline ring

Both TO aromatic rings are compared with guanine rings located in the upper and lower terminal G-tetrads.

The analysis evaluates:

- Distance between aromatic-ring centers
- Relative orientation of aromatic-ring planes
- Stacking occupancy for individual TO–guanine pairs
- Overall, upper-tetrad, and lower-tetrad stacking occupancy
- Mean and standard deviation across independent replicates

Trajectory frames may be sampled at regular intervals to reduce computational cost.
